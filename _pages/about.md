I am an **Assistant Professor** at the Institute of Environment and Power Technology (IEPT) at **Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET)**. My work sits at the intersection of thermal-fluid dynamics and computational materials science, with a primary focus on developing sustainable energy solutions and advanced diagnostic technologies.

## 🔬 Research Focus
My research journey began with experimental thermofluids, specifically investigating bubble dynamics and heat transfer enhancement. Over time, my focus has evolved toward understanding these phenomena at the atomic level through computational modeling. My current work includes:
*   **Computational Materials Science:** Utilizing large-scale Molecular Dynamics (MD) and Density Functional Theory (DFT) to study the mechanical and thermal properties of MXenes and High-Entropy Alloys (HEAs).
*   **Energy Materials:** Optimizing perovskite solar cells and developing simulation frameworks for solar-hydrogen systems.
*   **Microfluidics:** Exploring microfluidic hydraulic oscillators and acoustofluidics for cost-effective point-of-care diagnostics.
