# Mahmudul Hasan

I am an **Assistant Professor** at the Institute of Environment and Power Technology (IEPT) at **Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET)**[cite: 4, 11]. My work sits at the intersection of thermal-fluid dynamics and computational materials science, with a primary focus on developing sustainable energy solutions and advanced diagnostic technologies[cite: 11, 14].

## 🔬 Research Focus
My research journey began with experimental thermofluids, specifically investigating bubble dynamics and heat transfer enhancement[cite: 13, 14]. Over time, my focus has evolved toward understanding these phenomena at the atomic level through computational modeling[cite: 12]. My current work includes:
*   **Computational Materials Science:** Utilizing large-scale Molecular Dynamics (MD) and Density Functional Theory (DFT) to study the mechanical and thermal properties of MXenes and High-Entropy Alloys (HEAs)[cite: 11, 12].
*   **Energy Materials:** Optimizing perovskite solar cells and developing simulation frameworks for solar-hydrogen systems[cite: 11].
*   **Microfluidics:** Exploring microfluidic hydraulic oscillators and acoustofluidics for cost-effective point-of-care diagnostics[cite: 11].
